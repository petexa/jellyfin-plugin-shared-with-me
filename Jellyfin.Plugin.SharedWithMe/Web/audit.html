The share folder can't be changed while the plugin manages access. Remove every share and restore everyone below first.
Everyone who may share gets a private playlist "Share with <name>" for each profile with limits; adding a film or an episode there shares it in any app, removing it stops sharing. Turning this off removes those playlists; shares stay.
A TV app has no way to choose how long to share, so a title added to a "Share with" playlist is shared for this many days, then taken out of the playlist. 0 keeps it until it is removed. At most 366. Shares already made keep their own end date.
